Core Problem
------------
How should the structure of the physical world be represented in neural systems?

Modern AI systems increasingly interact with complex physical environments through sensors and actuators. 
A central challenge is to develop representations that capture the geometric and structural regularities of the physical world while remaining scalable, learnable, and usable for perception and interaction.


Research Questions
------------------
1. What forms of geometric structure should be explicitly encoded in neural representations of 3D scenes?

2. What representations enable neural scene models to scale with real-world sensing systems?

3. What representations enable AI systems to reason about and interact with the physical world?


Research Directions
-------------------
Geometric Representation Learning  
Studying how geometric structure (e.g., spatial relations, surfaces, topology) can be embedded in neural representations of 3D scenes.

Scalable Neural Scene Modeling  
Developing representations and learning frameworks that scale to real-world sensing systems with many cameras, large environments, and long time horizons.

Representation for Interaction  
Exploring how scene representations can support reasoning, planning, and interaction with the physical world.
